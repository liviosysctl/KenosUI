# Badge

Small non-interactive status label for counts, states and categories.

**Palettes** `primary` (neutral black/grey), `accent` (red — "new", "live", unread), and `info` · `success` · `warning` · `error` for states. **Variants** `subtle` (default), `solid`, `surface`, `outline`, `plain`. **Sizes** `xs` 16px · `sm` 20 (default) · `md` 24 · `lg` 28.

**Consumer provides** the short text (children). One or two words; numerals are tabular.

- Don't: make a badge clickable — use a ghost Button.
